Privacy Policy

Effective: October 31, 2026 • Last Updated: October 31, 2026 • Target: Android & Garmin Connect IQ
Navigation IQ works entirely offline. Turn guidance extracted from your navigation notifications is forwarded directly to your Garmin watch over Bluetooth. No route history, personal data, or usage metrics are recorded or transmitted off your device.

1. Overview

Navigation IQ is developed by PhucDNH as a companion utility application designed to mirror turn-by-turn navigation guidance from Google Maps onto paired Garmin smartwatches via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).

This Privacy Policy details the device permissions required for real-time turn forwarding and explains our complete data isolation model.

2. Device Access & Permissions

To detect active navigation instructions and transmit glanceable alerts to your paired Garmin wearable, Navigation IQ requests access to specific Android capabilities:

Notification Listener Access NotificationListenerService

Listens exclusively for active turn-by-turn navigation alerts posted by Google Maps. Direction text, maneuver icons, and distance countdowns are parsed dynamically in volatile memory (RAM) and packaged for Bluetooth transmission. Notification data is never stored on disk and never uploaded to any remote server.

Nearby Devices & Bluetooth BLUETOOTH_CONNECT

Required by the official Garmin Connect IQ Mobile SDK to scan for, establish, and maintain an encrypted communication session with your paired Garmin smartwatch.

Foreground Connected Service FOREGROUND_SERVICE

Maintains an active background transmission pipeline while navigation is ongoing, preventing Android power managers from terminating the watch bridge when your screen turns off.

Battery Optimization Exemption Optional

Requested optionally so the operating system does not throttle background BLE streaming during long drives or extended cycling rides.

3. Data Handling & Storage

Navigation IQ is built around an on-device processing model. The application does not maintain persistent trip logs or transmit information outside your personal devices:

  • Ephemeral Memory Processing: Turn-by-turn directions, distances, and road names extracted from active navigation notifications are held in volatile memory (RAM) solely while formatting payloads for the watch. Once sent over Bluetooth, these payloads are immediately dropped.
  • Zero Persistent History: Trip routes, waypoints, and timestamps are not written to local storage, app databases, or log files. When navigation ends, no trace of the route remains inside Navigation IQ.
  • No Account Requirements: The application functions independently without user accounts, logins, or cloud synchronization. It does not request or access personal identifiers such as names, phone numbers, or email addresses.
  • Direct Local Transfer: Traffic flows exclusively between your Android device and paired Garmin watch over local Bluetooth. The app contains no remote telemetry endpoints, third-party tracking libraries, or advertising frameworks.

4. Third-Party Developer Services

Navigation IQ integrates solely with the official wearable communication library required to interface with Garmin devices:

Garmin Connect IQ Mobile SDK Wearable Comm

Facilitates direct, encrypted Bluetooth transmission between your Android smartphone and paired Garmin wearable. Device interactions are governed by the Garmin Privacy Policy.

5. Security Architecture

Navigation IQ handles incoming navigation data entirely in volatile memory (RAM). Turn notices, distance metrics, and road titles are decoded, immediately forwarded to your Garmin device over authenticated Bluetooth BLE, and dropped from memory once transmitted.

Because no navigational logs are written to persistent device storage or uploaded across the internet, your travel data remains completely confidential on your personal hardware.

6. Children's Privacy

Navigation IQ is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal identifiable information from children.
